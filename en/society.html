The Society

Asian Society for Vascular Biology

The Asian Society for Vascular Biology (ASVB) is a non-profit scientific society founded in Hong Kong in 2004 at the initiative of Professor Paul M. Vanhoutte.

The Society’s members come from mainland China, Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan, as well as Japan, South Korea, Singapore and Malaysia.

Our mission

Bylaws and sources

01

Advance vascular biology and medicine

Advance vascular biology and medicine in Asia and uphold high standards in research, education and scientific exchange.

02

Support teaching and continuing education

Foster research and teaching in universities and research institutes and support continuing education for vascular researchers and practising physicians.

03

Connect researchers and clinicians

Bring basic scientists, clinicians and early-career researchers together through scientific meetings, the Young Scholar Forum and poster sessions.

04

Provide scientific advice

Advise educational institutions, government agencies and other organizations on relevant scientific matters when requested and as authorized by the Society.

Memorial lectures & early-career forum

Paul M. Vanhoutte Memorial Lecture

Honours Founding President Professor Paul M. Vanhoutte, a pioneer in vascular endothelium pharmacology. Delivered by Yu Huang in Nanjing (2024) and Nanping Wang in Singapore (2026).

Tony J. F. Lee Memorial Lecture

Established at the 2018 Shanghai General Assembly in memory of Professor Tony J. F. Lee, organizer of the 7th meeting in Hualien. Delivered by Alex F. Chen (2024) and Maik Gollasch (2025).

Young Scholar Forum & posters

Building on the Young Investigator Award introduced in Xi’an (2012), dedicated oral sessions and poster tracks support early-career investigators and postgraduate trainees.

Our history

From the Society’s founding in Hong Kong in 2004 to its scientific meetings across Asia.

Founding and inaugural meeting sources

Vascular myography workshop

On 16–17 May 2014, ASVB co-organized the Myography in Greater China workshop at Fudan University Shanghai Medical College. The workshop featured seven invited speakers who shared experimental methods for measuring vascular responses.

  1. 2004

    Society founded · Hong Kong

    Professor Paul M. Vanhoutte founded ASVB in Hong Kong to advance vascular biology and medicine in Asia.

  2. 2005

    1st · Shanghai

    The 1st Scientific Meeting of The Asian Society for Vascular Biology opened on 8 October 2005 in Shanghai, China. Founding President Paul M. Vanhoutte and Professor Ding-Feng Su organized the meeting. ASVB and the Second Military Medical University jointly invited delegates, and Professor Su headed the university’s Department of Pharmacology.

  3. 2006

    2nd · Shanghai

    The second scientific meeting returned to Shanghai, host city of the inaugural meeting in 2005.

  4. 2008

    3rd · Singapore

    The National University of Singapore hosted the third meeting.

  5. 2010

    4th · Hong Kong

    Hong Kong hosted the fourth scientific meeting.

  6. 2012

    5th · Xi’an

    Held on 27–29 October and organized by Nanping Wang and Zuyi Yuan, the meeting drew about 200 participants. Tony J. F. Lee delivered the fifth Asian Lecture on Vascular Biology. The programme included a Young Investigator Award competition, and the General Assembly elected Zuyi Yuan and Jeng-Jiann Chiu to the Council.

  7. 2014

    6th · Kuala Lumpur

    Held on 22–24 August and organized by Mohammad Rais Mustafa, the meeting brought together 18 invited speakers from 10 countries and regions. Hiroaki Shimokawa delivered the sixth Asian Lecture on Vascular Biology. The abstracts and a meeting editorial appeared in the Journal of Cardiovascular Pharmacology.

  8. 2016

    7th · Hualien

    Held on 27–29 October and organized by Tony J. F. Lee at Hualien Tzu Chi Hospital, the meeting drew 223 participants from 14 countries and regions and featured 35 posters. Jeng-Jiann Chiu delivered the seventh Asian Lecture on Vascular Biology.

  9. 2018

    8th · Shanghai

    Held on 2–4 November at the Yide Auditorium, Shanghai Jiao Tong University School of Medicine, alongside the third symposium of the vascular medicine committee of the Chinese Association of Pathophysiology. Yu Huang delivered the eighth Asian Lecture on Vascular Biology. At the 3 November General Assembly, the Society transitioned to new leadership and established the Tony J. F. Lee Memorial Lecture.

  10. 2024

    9th · Nanjing

    Welcomed around 200 participants; Yu Huang delivered the Paul M. Vanhoutte Memorial Lecture and Alex F. Chen delivered the Tony J. F. Lee Memorial Lecture.

  11. 2025

    10th · Shenzhen

    Joined the second Conference on Vascular Homeostasis and Diseases, with the General Assembly endorsing the 2025–2027 Society leadership and council transition.

  12. 2026

    11th · Singapore

    Returned to the National University of Singapore with the Paul M. Vanhoutte Memorial Lecture, Young Scholar Forum and adoption of the amended Council Bylaws.

  13. 2027

    12th · Macao (planned)

    Planned for 23–24 August 2027 at Macau University of Science and Technology, connecting the Greater Bay Area and wider Asia-Pacific vascular biology community.

Council governance & academic ties

Council size & two-year term

Under the Council Bylaws adopted on 12 June 2026, the Council comprises 10 to 30 elected Council Members alongside Co-opted Members, with the Honorary President serving ex-officio (non-voting). Officers and Council members serve two-year renewable terms.

Standing committees

The Bylaws provide for four standing committees—Scientific Program, Membership, Finance and Nominating—and require regular Council meetings ordinarily convened alongside the Scientific Meeting.

Academic & regional ties

ASVB maintains active scholarly exchange with regional peers including JVBMO (Japan) and KVBM (Korea), alongside academic publishing support from Vessel Plus.
