THE SOCIETY

Asian Society for Vascular Biology

ASVB advances research, education and scientific exchange in vascular biology and medicine in Asia. Its meetings bring researchers from universities, institutes and hospitals into the same conversation.

The Society’s members come from mainland China, Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan, as well as Japan, South Korea, Singapore and Malaysia.

SCIENTIFIC EXCHANGE

The work of the Society

Scientific sessions connect work on the endothelium, vascular muscle and surrounding tissues with clinical questions about mechanisms and treatment. Named lectures, focused talks and posters give members several ways to discuss their findings.

The Society supports the teaching of vascular biology and continuing education for scientists and physicians. The Young Scholar Forum gives postgraduate students and early-career investigators a place to present and discuss their research.

MEETING HISTORY

Meeting history

  1. 2005

    Shanghai

    The inaugural scientific meeting brought basic scientists and clinicians together to discuss vascular biology and disease.

  2. 2008

    Singapore

    The National University of Singapore hosted the third meeting. In 2026 the Society returned to the same MD11 Auditorium for its 11th meeting.

  3. 2018

    Shanghai

    The eighth meeting was held jointly with the third symposium of the vascular medicine committee of the Chinese Association of Pathophysiology.

  4. 2025

    Shenzhen

    The 10th ASVB meeting joined the second Conference on Vascular Homeostasis and Diseases.

  5. 2026

    Singapore

    The 11th meeting featured the Paul M. Vanhoutte Memorial Lecture and a Young Scholar Forum.

  6. 2027

    Macao · planned

    Macau University of Science and Technology is the proposed venue for the 12th meeting.
