The Society

Asian Society for Vascular Biology

The Asian Society for Vascular Biology (ASVB) is a non-profit scientific society founded in Hong Kong in 2004 at the initiative of Professor Paul M. Vanhoutte.

The Society’s members come from mainland China, Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan, as well as Japan, South Korea, Singapore and Malaysia.

Our mission

Bylaws and sources

Advance vascular biology and medicine

Advance vascular biology and medicine in Asia and uphold high standards in research, education and scientific exchange.

Support teaching and continuing education

Foster research and teaching in universities and research institutes and support continuing education for vascular researchers and practising physicians.

Connect researchers and clinicians

Bring basic scientists, clinicians and early-career researchers together through scientific meetings, the Young Scholar Forum and poster sessions.

Provide scientific advice

Advise educational institutions, government agencies and other organizations on relevant scientific matters when requested and as authorized by the Society.

Our history

From the Society’s founding in 2004 to its scientific meetings across Asia.

  1. 2004

    Society founded · Hong Kong

    Established at the initiative of Professor Paul M. Vanhoutte to advance vascular biology and medicine in Asia.

  2. 2005

    1st · Shanghai

    The inaugural meeting brought together basic and clinical researchers across the Asia-Pacific.

  3. 2006

    2nd · China

    After the inaugural meeting in Shanghai, the Society held its second scientific meeting in China.

  4. 2008

    3rd · Singapore

    The National University of Singapore hosted the third meeting.

  5. 2010

    4th · Hong Kong

    Hong Kong hosted the fourth scientific meeting.

  6. 2012

    5th · Xi’an

    The Xi’an meeting featured a Young Investigator Award and a General Assembly.

  7. 2014

    6th · Kuala Lumpur

    Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, hosted the sixth meeting.

  8. 2016

    7th · Hualien

    Hualien Tzu Chi Hospital hosted the seventh meeting.

  9. 2018

    8th · Shanghai

    The eighth meeting was held jointly with the third symposium of the vascular medicine committee of the Chinese Association of Pathophysiology.

  10. 2024

    9th · Nanjing

    Professor Yu Huang delivered the Paul M. Vanhoutte Memorial Lecture.

  11. 2025

    10th · Shenzhen

    The 10th ASVB meeting joined the second Conference on Vascular Homeostasis and Diseases.

  12. 2026

    11th · Singapore

    The meeting returned to the National University of Singapore and featured the Paul M. Vanhoutte Memorial Lecture and a Young Scholar Forum.

  13. 2027

    12th · Macao (planned)

    Macau University of Science and Technology is the proposed venue.

Presidents and Secretaries-General

Presidents

  1. Founding years Paul M. Vanhoutte

    Founding President, in office at the first scientific meeting in 2005.

  2. 2010–2018 Yu Huang

    Previously the founding Secretary-General, he became President in 2010.

  3. 2018–2025 Nanping Wang

    Elected in Shanghai in 2018 and chaired the 2025 General Assembly in Shenzhen before the leadership transition.

  4. 2025–2027 Jinsong Bian

    The 2025 General Assembly in Shenzhen endorsed his appointment for the current term.

Secretaries-General

  1. 2005–2010 Yu Huang

    Founding Secretary-General.

  2. Served before 2018 Nanping Wang

    His Xi’an Jiaotong University profile records his service in this role. Exact dates remain to be verified.

  3. Elected in 2018 Jeng-Jiann Chiu

    The Shanghai General Assembly approved a 2018–2020 term. His exact departure date remains to be verified.

  4. 2025–2027 Susan W.S. Leung

    Endorsed for the 2025–2027 term in Shenzhen and listed as Secretary-General in the 2026 Singapore minutes.
