First announcement

The 12th Scientific Meeting of the Asian Society for Vascular Biology is planned to be held at Macau University of Science and Technology, Macao, China, on 23–24 August 2027. The meeting will bring together researchers and clinicians to discuss advances in vascular biology and vascular medicine through scientific lectures, a Young Scholar Forum and poster presentations.

Hosted in Macao, the meeting aims to strengthen scholarly exchange between the Guangdong–Hong Kong–Macao Greater Bay Area and the wider Asia-Pacific vascular biology community. We warmly invite researchers, clinicians, early-career investigators and postgraduate students to attend and share their work.

Call for abstracts

We welcome abstracts on vascular biology, mechanisms of vascular disease and related translational research for oral or poster presentation. Early-career researchers and postgraduate students are encouraged to submit abstracts and participate in the Young Scholar Forum.

  • Endothelial biology & homeostasis
  • Vascular remodelling & aortic disease
  • Cardiometabolic & immunovascular axes
  • Imaging, omics & disease models
  • Clinical & translational frontiers
  • Early-career training & exchange

Please use the abstract template and include the title, authors, affiliations, abstract text, keywords and corresponding author details. An English abstract is recommended. Indicate your preferred presentation format on the attendance intention form. Acceptance and presentation arrangements will be communicated by the Organizing Committee.
